# Analysis Plan

## Data-quality checks
- Confirm `participant_id` is unique.
- Check required fields for blanks.
- Confirm score values are between 0 and 10.
- Standardise province and Yes/No categories.
- Check that follow-up completion is logically consistent with available scores.
- Record exclusions and corrections rather than silently changing source data.

## Measures
- Total eligible participants.
- Number and percentage with a completed follow-up.
- Response rate = completed follow-ups / eligible participants.
- Average pre-score and average post-score among records with both scores.
- Average score change for paired records only.

## Reporting cautions
- State the denominator and number of valid records for each metric.
- Do not interpret missing responses as zero.
- Do not claim causal impact from pre/post differences alone.
- The sample data is fictional and must not be presented as actual programme outcomes.
